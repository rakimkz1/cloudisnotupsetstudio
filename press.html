Seal way

Seal way is a cozy 3D adventure where you play as a young seal trying to find its way home after being captured and taken to an aquarium.

Explore the aquarium, solve puzzles, avoid dangers, and find your way home. We offer you a colorful world, mesmerizing scenery, and much more!
